## Report: F118 Flaky tests

- **Status:** Done (awaiting merge).
- **Summary:**
  - `RangeComment` lost the text of its third comment box in about 1 of 10 suite runs. The cause is in `@pierre/diffs` 1.5.2 (and 1.6.4): a window scroll in the frame that makes the file taller is handled with the old page height and the old file height, so the virtualizer renders no rows for one frame. That render removes the slot of the comment box, and the focused textarea loses the focus to `body` for about 30 ms. `FileView` of `CodeFile.tsx` now measures again on a scroll that sees a new page height, through the public `reconcileHeights()` and `markDOMDirty()`. Users no longer lose keystrokes in a new comment box.
  - `CodeView` "Ctrl or Meta click": headless Chromium opens no tab for about 1 in 75 such clicks, although the page does its part in each of them. The test now checks the part of the page.
  - `ProposalInFileView` (F62 candidate): not reproduced in 600 runs at a load of 85 to 96. No change.
  - No other flaky test in 10 runs of the UI suite, 3 runs of the PHP suites, 3 runs of the e2e suite, and 5 runs of Vitest.
- **Files:**
  - Changed: `assets/code/CodeFile.tsx` (effect `followPageHeight` in `FileView`), `tests/ui/CodeView.spec.ts` (Ctrl or Meta click test, helper `recordNextClick`), `docs/plan/README.md` (two Known gotchas of the mocked UI suite).
  - Created: `tests/ui/CommentBoxFocus.spec.ts`, `docs/plan/reports/F118.md`.
- **Tests added:**
  - UI: `CommentBoxFocus` "A comment box that opens below two new threads keeps the focus while the window scrolls to it".
  - UI, changed: `CodeView` "A file in the tree is a link: a Ctrl or Meta click is left to the browser, and a click opens it in place".
- **Measurements:**
  - UI suite `--repeat-each=10` on `7f4797b4`, load about 8: 2569 passed, 1 failed (`RangeComment`, third `fill()` with "Add comment" disabled).
  - `RangeComment` alone: 40 of 40 at load 8, 48 of 48 with 24 `yes` processes and 12 workers. The failure needs `fill()` inside the 30 ms without focus.
  - Logs in the page and in the virtualizer, flow of `RangeComment`, before the fix: in each of 24 runs the textarea got `focusout` to `null` about 20 ms after the focus, after `applyFullRender` replaced the rows. Values of one run: page height 1439 to 1781 px, `scrollY` 156 to 1061 px, virtualizer scroll top 719 px (clamped to the old page height), file height 629.5 px (1362.5 px measured later), render range `totalLines: 0`.
  - With only `markDOMDirty()` on the scroll: scroll top 1061 px, file height still 629.5 px, still no rows. With `reconcileHeights()` as well: no `focusout`, no empty render, no removed slot in 8 of 8 runs.
  - `CommentBoxFocus` without the change: 8 of 8 failed (5 `focusout` events, 4 expected), on `7f4797b4` and again after the rebase onto F119. With the change: 8 of 8 passed.
  - `CodeView` Ctrl or Meta click with logs: 16 of 1200 runs on 4 workers at load 14 to 22 opened no tab, also after 8 s more. In each failure and each pass the click was trusted, on the link, with `metaKey` true, `navigator.userActivation.isActive` true, not default-prevented, and the page stayed on its file. The failures happened at the same times after the page load as the passes (363 to 440 ms, passes 345 to 681 ms).
  - Changed `CodeView` test: 300 of 300 passed. With the tree item mutated to prevent a Ctrl or Meta click, it fails ("Expected: false, Received: true").
  - `ProposalInFileView` `--repeat-each=150` on 8 workers: 300 of 300 with the change at load 89 to 96, 300 of 300 without it at load about 85.
  - UI suite `--repeat-each=10` with both changes, after the rebase onto F119, load 69 at the start and 26 at the end: 2630 of 2630 passed in 7.1 min.
  - Vitest 5 times: 133 of 133 each.
  - `make test` 3 times at load 8 to 20: OK, 2245 tests and 22659 assertions each. `make e2e` 3 times at load 11 to 17: OK, 14 tests and 326 assertions each.
  - During this feature, the F121 `check-main` of a peer on `main` failed once on the same `RangeComment` line (third box empty after `fill()`), and passed on the second run.
- **Verification:** `make check` on the branch rebased onto `2beb97cc`: exit 0 in 5 min 17 s. cs and lint clean, PHPStan no errors, PHPUnit 2245 tests and 22659 assertions, Vitest 133 passed, UI 264 passed, e2e 14 tests and 326 assertions. `make act` did not run: a green `make act` had seen the same workflow inputs (F120).
- **Decisions and deviations:**
  - The scope file records the decisions: the code view change, the CodeView test, and ProposalInFileView without a change.
  - The search for a Chromium or Playwright issue about dropped Ctrl or Meta tabs found nothing specific.
- **Follow-ups and new ideas:**
  - Report the clamp of `@pierre/diffs` upstream: `Virtualizer.clampScrollTop()` uses the page height of the last resize notification, and `VirtualizedFile` uses the file height of its last render, so a scroll in the frame that grows the file renders no rows. For Jan, if he wants to.
