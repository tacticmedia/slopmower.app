## Report: F46 RDS auth bundle 1.2

- **Status:** Done (awaiting commit).
- **Summary:**
  - `tacticmedia/rds-auth-bundle` 1.2.0 and `tacticmedia/rds-auth-middleware` 1.2.0 replace 1.1.2 and 1.1.3. No other package changed in `composer.lock`.
  - The accepted credentials live in the dedicated filesystem pool `rds_credentials.cache` instead of `cache.app`. The pool shares `framework.cache.directory` with `cache.app`, so the `php` and `worker` containers share it, but its own namespace keeps it apart from application data. With 1.2.0 the master password stays in the pool until the next rotation.
  - `config/packages/rds_auth.yaml` sets `cache_pool` only. Every other option keeps its default.
- **Files:**
  - Packages: `composer.json` (`^1.2`), `composer.lock`.
  - Configuration: `config/packages/cache.yaml`, `config/packages/rds_auth.yaml` (new).
  - Docs: `docs/architecture.md` (D58), `docs/deployment.md` (credential cache), `docs/plan/features.md` (F46 row, the "For Jan" item "RDS bundle 1.2.0" removed).
  - Created: `docs/plan/F46-rds-auth-bundle-1-2.md`, `docs/plan/reports/F46.md`.
- **Tests added:** none. The existing `RdsAuthenticationTest` covers the middleware on the 1.2.0 packages: the IAM sign-in of the lock store and of the Messenger queue, the raw password, and the `ConfiguredPasswordOutdated` listener. The pool choice is configuration that `debug:container` shows.
- **Verification:**
  - `composer require tacticmedia/rds-auth-bundle:^1.2 --with-dependencies`: the lock changes only the bundle (1.1.2 to 1.2.0) and the middleware (1.1.3 to 1.2.0). `composer show` gives 1.2.0 for both.
  - `debug:config rds_auth`: `cache_pool: rds_credentials.cache`, every other option at its default. `debug:container rds_auth.cache_pool`: lazy, factory `current`, argument `rds_credentials.cache`. `debug:container rds_credentials.cache`: a `TraceableAdapter` with the tag `cache.pool`.
  - After `make worker-restart`, the worker consumes `async`. `dbal:run-sql "SELECT current_user"` gives `app`, and the login page shows "Sign in as jan".
  - `RdsAuthenticationTest` on the 1.2.0 packages: 4 of 4.
  - `make check` with `make act`, `HTTP_PORT=8138` and `DATABASE_PUBLISHED_PORT=5538`: `make cs` 0 of 751 files to fix, `make stan` no errors, `make lint` OK, `make test` OK with 1757 tests and 17329 assertions, `make front` 27 files and 90 tests, `make ui` 126 passed, `make e2e` OK with 10 tests. The act job succeeded with the same gates, `composer validate`, `composer audit` (no advisories), and `npm audit` (0 vulnerabilities). 9 min 51 s.
  - `composer validate` warns about `ergebnis/composer-normalize` (`*`) and `symfony/reprise` (`1.3.0`). Both warnings existed before F46.
- **Decisions and deviations:**
  - The dedicated pool is a decision of this feature beyond the version bump. With 1.2.0 the pool holds the master password until the next rotation, and the bundle documentation recommends a dedicated filesystem pool for one host with web workers and CLI processes. APCu would not reach the worker, because every CLI process has its own segment.
  - F28 rejected APCu partly because the middleware constructed its pool also in pass-through mode. Bundle 1.2.0 constructs it lazily, so that reason is gone. The worker argument above still rules APCu out.
- **Versions:** `tacticmedia/rds-auth-bundle` 1.2.0 and `tacticmedia/rds-auth-middleware` 1.2.0, the latest on Packagist on 2026-10-09.
- **Follow-ups and open questions:** none.
- **Risks discovered:** the pool stores the credential unencrypted on local disk, as `cache.app` did. `docs/deployment.md` states where it lives and who must read it.
- **Next:** the first entry of [features.md](../features.md) that is not Done, after the known issues.
