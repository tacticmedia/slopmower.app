## Report: F95 Markdown editor with Write and Preview

- **Status:** Done 2026-11-03.
- **Summary:**
  - `MarkdownEditor` replaces the `Textarea` of `CommentBox`. It has the tabs **Write** and **Preview** (Primer `UnderlinePanels`), a toolbar (Primer `ActionBar` of `IconButton`s: Heading, Bold, Italic, Quote, Code, Link, Bulleted list, Numbered list, Task list), and the text field.
  - `@github/markdown-toolbar-element` 2.2.3 is installed with an exact pin. Each button and shortcut clicks a hidden `<md-*>` element of `<markdown-toolbar for="<textarea id>" hidden>`, so the edit goes through `execCommand('insertText')` and undo removes it. A JSX `IntrinsicElements` declaration for the custom elements is in `MarkdownEditor.tsx`.
  - `UnderlinePanels` keeps the inactive panel mounted: `Panel` always renders and gets `hidden` from `useTabPanel` (`dist/experimental/Tabs/useTabPanel.js`). So the text field, its ref, and its undo history stay while **Preview** shows. Preview renders `MarkdownBody` lazily, or "Nothing to preview" for a blank body.
  - Keys in the text field: Mod+B, Mod+I, Mod+E, Mod+K, Mod+Shift+7, Mod+Shift+8, and Mod+Shift+. run the matching button. Mod+Shift+P anywhere in the editor switches the tab and moves the focus to the **Preview** tab or back to the text field. Mod+K in the field inserts a link; quick open already ignores Mod+K in an editable element.
  - Tab and Shift+Tab with a non-empty selection indent or dedent the selected lines by 2 spaces through `indentLines()` (`assets/comments/indentLines.ts`) and `execCommand('insertText')`; the lines stay selected. Without a selection, Tab moves the focus.
  - `CommentBox` owns the tab state: `quote()` and a saved comment switch back to **Write**. In a box with a title, `FormControl id` is the id of the text field, so the **Description** label still names it.
  - `isPressed()` also matches `KeyboardEvent.code`, so `Digit7`, `Digit8`, and `Period` match whatever character Shift and the layout give.
  - The formatting keys and buttons do nothing while the box is busy (read-only).
- **Files:** `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, `assets/comments/MarkdownEditor.tsx` (new), `assets/comments/MarkdownEditor.module.css` (new), `assets/comments/indentLines.ts` (new), `assets/comments/indentLines.test.ts` (new), `assets/comments/CommentBox.tsx`, `assets/layout/modShortcut.ts`, `tests/ui/MarkdownEditor.spec.ts` (new), `tests/ui/FileComment.spec.ts`, `tests/ui/QuickOpen.spec.ts`, `docs/user/comment.md`, `docs/user/keyboard-shortcuts.md`.
- **Tests:**
  - Vitest `IndentLinesTest`: "Indents and dedents every line of a multi-line selection", "Dedent removes a tab or 1 or 2 leading spaces", "Dedent leaves a line without indentation unchanged", "A selection that ends at the start of a line leaves that line alone".
  - UI `MarkdownEditor`: "Bold wraps the selection in **", "Tab with a selection over 2 lines indents both, and Shift+Tab dedents them", "Tab without a selection moves the focus to the next control", "Preview shows **x** in bold, and Write shows the text again", "Mod+Shift+P toggles Write and Preview", "Mod+K in the field inserts a link and opens no quick open".
- **Verification:**
  - `make front`: 31 files, 112 tests passed. `make ui`: 211 passed. `MarkdownEditor.spec.ts`: 6 passed.
  - Mutation checks: a dedent that removes only 2 spaces fails "Dedent removes a tab or 1 or 2 leading spaces". Link without its Mod+K shortcut fails "Mod+K in the field inserts a link and opens no quick open". Both restored.
  - By hand in Playwright: undo after Bold and after Tab restores the text; Mod+Shift+8, Mod+Shift+7, and Mod+Shift+. format the selected lines; Shift+Tab from the field reaches the toolbar; the **Description** label names the field.
  - `CodeBrowser` journey capture "Comment box with a title field" shows the tabs and the toolbar. In the narrow reply boxes at 768 px, `ActionBar` moves the last buttons into its "More items" menu.
  - `make check` not run: this feature is front-end only, and the Docker targets were out of bounds for this run. `DocsRenderingTest` (PHP) did not run against the changed user docs.
- **Deviations:**
  - Two existing comment specs changed. `FileComment` "Escape cancels the comment box, ..." lists the buttons of the form; it now filters to buttons with text, because the toolbar buttons are buttons too. `QuickOpen` "Cmd+K in a text field does not open Quick open" types `x` after Mod+K in a comment box and now expects `[x](url)`; it still checks that no dialog opens.
  - `assets/layout/modShortcut.ts` changed (not listed in scope): `isPressed()` also matches `KeyboardEvent.code`. Without it, Mod+Shift+7 gives `&` on a US layout and never matches `7`.
  - A selection that ends at the start of a line does not indent that line; Primer's removed `_useIndenting.ts` indents it. Selecting 2 lines with Shift+Down ends there, and the scope says "the selected lines". One more Vitest case covers it.
  - The toolbar is inside the **Write** panel, above the text field, right-aligned, not on the row of the tabs as on GitHub. `UnderlinePanels` has no slot in its tab row, and a toolbar after the tabs in the DOM but before them on screen breaks the focus order.
- **Follow-ups and open questions:**
  - Candidate: show each shortcut in the tooltip of its button (`IconButton keybindingHint`), as GitHub does.
  - Candidate: Mod+Enter, Escape, and the formatting shortcuts in `NoteDialog`, `SubmitFeedbackOverlay`, and `CloseReviewDialog` (out of scope).
- **Risks discovered:**
  - `markdown-toolbar-element` caches one failed `execCommand` in a module variable and from then on sets `textarea.value` directly, without undo and without a React change event. A read-only field causes such a failure, so the editor does not format while the box is busy. Chromium, which the UI suite uses, supports `execCommand('insertText')` on a textarea; other browsers are not checked.
  - `ActionBar` measures its overflow with an observer. While **Preview** shows, all toolbar buttons count as overflowing; for about one frame after the switch back, "More items" can take the focus of a Shift+Tab and then disappear. A person cannot press that fast; a test can.
